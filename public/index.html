    LEADERBOARD

      BLANION is an independent fan-made game about Blanchardstown & Dublin 15. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

      HOW TO PLAY

      Every day, everyone gets the same seven questions about Blanchardstown. You have 25 seconds for each one.

      Any correct answer scores — but the fewer divers who give the same answer, the more it is worth. Obvious answers sink to the bottom of the pile. Deep cuts float your little angler all the way down to the abyss.

      Scores are provisional while the day is live (based on expected popularity, blended with real answers as they arrive). When the day closes at midnight, rarity is locked in from actual answer counts and everyone is scored the same way.

      Answered something we don't recognise? It is never thrown away: it goes to the Harbour Masters for verification, and if it is approved your score updates automatically.
